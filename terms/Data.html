We Don't Store, Keep, or Copy Your Data

Learn how CoolkidLabs handles your information with respect and transparency.

01

No Data Storage or Collection

CoolkidLabs does not store, keep, copy, or retain any data that you input into our tools or services. When you paste a chat, upload content, or use any CoolkidLabs feature, your data remains entirely yours and is processed only in your browser. No copies are kept on our servers.

02

How Your Information is Processed

All processing occurs client-side in your web browser. When you export, download, or convert content, the operation happens locally on your device. We do not log, track, or save the content you process. Any data transmitted to our servers (such as for public link importing) is processed temporarily and discarded immediately after serving your request.

03

Cookies and Tracking

CoolkidLabs does not use cookies, trackers, or analytics that identify you personally. We do not collect browsing history, usage patterns, or any personally identifiable information. Your privacy is protected by design.

04

Third-Party Platforms

When you import content from third-party platforms (such as ChatGPT, Gemini, or other AI services) via public share links, CoolkidLabs only temporarily accesses publicly available information. We do not store this data or associate it with any user account. The content is retrieved on-demand and discarded after processing.

05

Your Responsibility

You are solely responsible for the content you export or process through CoolkidLabs tools. By using this service, you confirm that:

  • You have the legal right to access and export the content in question.
  • Your use complies with all applicable local, national, and international laws.
  • You will not use this tool for any unlawful, harmful, or unauthorised purpose.
  • You understand that publicly shared links were made public by their original author.
  • You hold CoolkidLabs harmless for any misuse of the exported content.
06

Data Breach and Security

Since we do not store your data, the risk of data breaches is minimal. However, we recommend using CoolkidLabs over secure internet connections and taking appropriate precautions when handling sensitive information. Your device's security is your responsibility.

07

Changes to This Privacy Policy

CoolkidLabs reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the service after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
